Privacy Policy

How MUZI protects your privacy with zero telemetry, zero accounts, and local storage.

Core Guarantee: MUZI Music is built with Zero Telemetry. We do not track your listening activity, collect personal identifiers, sell your data, or display any advertisements. Ever.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how MUZI Music ("we", "our", or "the Application") treats user data when you use the MUZI PC Desktop Edition (Windows & Linux) or Mobile applications. MUZI is an open-source, non-profit community project dedicated to providing an uncompromised, ad-free music listening experience.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Unlike commercial music streaming platforms, MUZI operates on an absolute minimal-exposure model:

  • No User Accounts: You do not need to register, provide an email address, username, phone number, or password.
  • No Listening History Tracking: We do not log what songs, artists, or genres you play or search for on any server.
  • No Fingerprinting or Telemetry: No background trackers, no crash analytics beacons, no Google Analytics, and no marketing SDKs are included in our code.
  • No Financial Data: The application is completely free and open source. We never request credit cards, payment methods, or billing details.

3. Local Storage & Offline Crates

All your personalized data remains strictly on your local computer or device:

  • Playlists & Favorites: Stored locally in your application cache/database folder.
  • Offline Audio Crates: Downloaded or cached audio files are stored in your device's designated local storage directory. You have 100% control to inspect or delete these files at any time.
  • Audio Equalizer Settings: Presets and volume preferences remain local.

4. Network Requests & Stream Providers

To deliver music playback and synchronized lyrics, the application communicates directly with public, open media endpoints:

  • Audio Streams: Media is fetched directly via open audio APIs and streaming protocols. These requests contain standard network metadata (such as your IP address) required for HTTP packet delivery, but MUZI attaches no tracking cookies or identity tags.
  • Synchronized Lyrics: Lyrics are fetched dynamically from public open-source lyrics repositories (such as LRCLIB).
  • GitHub Updates: The app checks GitHub Releases periodically to notify you when a new version is available.

5. Open Source Auditing

Because MUZI is 100% open source, any developer or security researcher can review, audit, and compile our source code independently to verify these privacy claims. You can inspect the complete repository at github.com/biikkkuuuu/muzi-music-pc.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on our GitHub repository or contact the lead maintainer at @biikkkuuuu.
