    🎵 App & Features

    What is MUZI Music?

    MUZI is a 100% ad-free, open-source music streaming and playback application designed for pure acoustic enjoyment. It delivers lossless audio streaming, real-time multilingual synchronized lyrics, offline crate downloads, and zero privacy tracking on Android and PC.

    Is MUZI 100% free forever? Are there any subscriptions or hidden fees?

    Yes! MUZI is completely free and open-source under the MIT license. There are no monthly subscriptions, no paywalled features, and absolutely zero audio or visual advertisements. Every feature is accessible to all users for free.

    Does MUZI support background playback with the screen locked?

    Yes, uninterrupted background playback is a core feature. You can lock your phone screen or browse other apps while your audio continues seamlessly. MUZI provides full lock-screen media controls, persistent notification widgets, and supports Bluetooth headset button gestures.

    Can I import my existing playlists from Spotify or YouTube?

    Yes! MUZI includes a built-in playlist importer. Simply copy and paste any public Spotify or YouTube playlist link into the app, and MUZI will match and populate all tracks directly into your personal crate within seconds.

    💻 PC & Downloads

    Where can I download the official MUZI Android app?

    You can download the latest official APK directly from our GitHub Releases page. Every release is cryptographically signed and built directly from the open-source codebase.

    Why is the APK hosted on GitHub instead of Google Play Store?

    Distributing via GitHub allows us to maintain 100% independence from commercial store restrictions, roll out new features immediately without approval delays, and guarantee that no proprietary tracking SDKs or ad frameworks are bundled into the binary.

    How do I install the APK on my Android phone?

    1. Tap the "Download for Android" button to download the .apk file.
    2. Open your notification drawer or Downloads folder and tap the downloaded APK.
    3. When prompted by Android, tap Settings and enable Allow from this source.
    4. Tap Install. Once completed, tap Open and enjoy 100% ad-free music.

    When will the PC Desktop edition be ready?

    The desktop edition is actively developed using Kotlin Compose Multiplatform for Windows 10/11, macOS, and Linux. You can view progress, preview developer builds, and star the repository on our GitHub repo. Official standalone desktop binaries will be released soon!

    🎧 Audio & Lyrics

    Which audio codecs and file formats are supported?

    MUZI supports both studio-grade lossless formats (FLAC, ALAC, WAV, AIFF up to 24-bit/192kHz) and high-efficiency compressed formats (MP3 at 320kbps, AAC, OGG Vorbis, and Opus) with automatic hardware-accelerated decoding.

    How do real-time synchronized lyrics work?

    When playing any song, tap or swipe up on the player screen to reveal karaoke-style synchronized lyrics. The text highlights dynamically in sync with the vocals. Tapping any line of the lyric immediately seeks audio playback to that exact verse.

    Are lyrics available in multiple languages?

    Yes! MUZI automatically fetches synced lyrics across regional and international languages including Hindi, Punjabi, Bhojpuri, Haryanvi, English, Spanish, Korean, Japanese, and more.

    Does MUZI feature an audio equalizer?

    Yes, MUZI includes an integrated graphic equalizer with bass boost, vocal clarity filters, acoustic staging enhancements, and pre-tuned presets for different musical genres and headphone types.

    💾 Offline Crates

    What are Offline Crates?

    Offline Crates are MUZI's built-in local caching system that stores audio files directly onto your device's internal storage or SD card. Once saved to a crate, tracks play instantly offline without requiring any cellular data or Wi-Fi connection.

    Do downloaded offline songs expire or require periodic check-ins?

    Never. Commercial streaming services revoke downloads if you do not connect online every 30 days. In MUZI, your downloaded music has no DRM locks, never expires, and remains permanently playable on your device.

    Can MUZI play local songs already stored in my phone's storage?

    Yes! MUZI automatically scans and indexes existing audio files from your device folders and SD card, organizing them by Artist, Album, and Folder with full embedded tag and album artwork support.

    🔒 Privacy & Security

    Does MUZI track my listening habits or collect analytics?

    Zero tracking. MUZI adheres to strict Zero Telemetry principles. No listening history, search terms, device identifiers, or IP addresses are logged, monitored, or transmitted to any server. Read our full Privacy Policy.

    Do I need an account or email to use MUZI?

    No account, phone number, or email is required. You simply install the application and enjoy your music immediately with total anonymity.

    How can I review the source code or report a bug?

    The entire source code is openly hosted on GitHub. Anyone can review the code, file bug reports on GitHub Issues, suggest enhancements, or contribute pull requests.
